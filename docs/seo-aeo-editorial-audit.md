## Sources and factual limits

Official sources were checked and linked next to the specific guidance they support. Most article content consists of original practical inspection and operating recommendations, not unsupported claims of observed customer results. Sources are used within their actual scope:

- [Palm Desert landscape maintenance guidance](https://www.palmdesert.gov/community/landscaping-and-park-maintenance) and [CVWD water-saving guidance](https://www.cvwd.org/667/Water-Saving-Guidelines): local conservation and runoff context; readers check their own provider and current requirements.
- [NWS August 2021 storm report](https://www.weather.gov/psr/StormReportfor3031August2021): a documented Coachella Valley dust/outflow example, not a diagnosis for every local installation.
- [EPA registered-disinfectant directions](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-registration/selected-epa-registered-disinfectants) and [EPA definitions of cleaning, sanitizing, and disinfecting](https://www.epa.gov/coronavirus-and-disinfectants/whats-difference-between-products-disinfect-sanitize-and-clean): distinguish claims and relevant product directions.
- [CDC facility cleaning guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/hygiene/about/when-and-how-to-clean-and-disinfect-a-facility.html) and [CDC athletic-facility guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/mrsa/prevention/coaches-athletic-directors.html): general facility and hygiene context with setting-specific limits, not a diagnosis or proof about a particular turf installation.
- [CPSC playground burn-awareness guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/s3fs-public/3200.pdf) and [Public Playground Safety Handbook](https://www.cpsc.gov/Playground-Handbook): separate surface-condition and inspection considerations; no particular turf system is certified by the articles.
- [San Francisco Bay Water Board stormwater overview](https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/rwqcb2/water_issues/programs/stormwater/): regional context; no article treats it as approval for a particular wash-water discharge.

The repository does not establish every property's turf specification, product selected for a future visit, customer outcome, governing document, or provider credential. The revised articles no longer depend on asserting those facts. They explain what the customer and provider should identify for the actual job. No known blog editorial repair is being left pending that evidence.
